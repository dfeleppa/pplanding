Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ (W3C Recommendation, 12 December 2024). Scope: public website and complete customer processes.

Every criterion starts UNVERIFIED. Local fixes or narrow structural checks do not establish site-wide success. For each row record applicability, route/state coverage, browser and assistive technology, test steps/results, date, and evidence path. Mark not applicable only after checking all content and external flows.

## Conformance gates

- Full pages, including all states and embedded content.
- Complete processes, including external booking steps.
- Accessibility-supported technologies and non-interference.
- Published production behavior matches tested source.
- Legal applicability and operational accessibility support reviewed.
